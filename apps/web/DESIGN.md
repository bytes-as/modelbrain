# ModelBrain web design

**Territory.** The licence your model actually inherited. A model card is a claim on paper; the
graph is the evidence. The site is a legal document that the graph redlines.

**Rule.** Every section is a clause of that licence: set on paper as the card claims it, then
corrected in red by what FalkorDB proves.

| Block       | In the rule's world                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nav         | Document header: § numbered clauses, live "In force" status from `/api/health`                                                                                                      |
| Hero        | The card's licence is struck through in red and the inherited licence written in the margin (real examples, cycling when idle); the visitor fills the blank with their own model id |
| Lineage     | § Inheritance: the chain of title from the model to the licence that binds it                                                                                                       |
| Weight DNA  | Exhibit A on the evidence ground: what the weights prove, real separation numbers                                                                                                   |
| Findings    | Schedule 1: real rows from `/api/dna/hidden`, live                                                                                                                                  |
| Memory      | Amendment: a dated change upstream and who did not receive notice (Xkev)                                                                                                            |
| Compounding | Countersignature: two companies sign, the trust tier rises                                                                                                                          |
| Agents / CI | Obligations of the agent: "shall call `can_i_use` before `from_pretrained`"                                                                                                         |
| FalkorDB    | Governing engine clause, (a)-(f)                                                                                                                                                    |
| FAQ         | Definitions                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Close       | Signature line, full redline band                                                                                                                                                   |
| Footer      | Signature block and the boilerplate: automated screening, not legal advice                                                                                                          |

**Grounds.** Paper `#f3efe6` and ink `#16161a` for claims; graphite `#121416` for evidence (the app
pages are the evidence room). One redline red `#d6332f`, spent only on contradictions and the close.

**Type.** Newsreader (display, light, roman + italic) · Inter Tight (text) · IBM Plex Mono (data).

**Owned ground avoided** (Oct 2026 audit of FOSSA, Endor Labs, Snyk, Prisma AIRS / Protect AI):
"control/secure your supply chain", governance language, gradient backgrounds, shields, logo strips,
dashboard screenshots. None of them shows the clause or the lineage itself.

**Facts.** Numbers come from the live API or are dated "as of 18 Oct 2026". Examples are real public
Hugging Face models; wording says "metadata and weights disagree", never accusations.
