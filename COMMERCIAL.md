## When you need a commercial licence

You need one if you commercialise ModelBrain. That includes:

- selling the software, or selling access to it
- offering it as a hosted or paid service
- shipping it inside a product you charge for
- using it in a revenue-generating company operation

Reading the code, running it locally to learn, academic or personal research, and noncommercial organisations are already covered by the noncommercial licence. When a use is partly commercial, treat it as commercial.

## What the commercial licence is

It is royalty-bearing. The royalty is agreed in writing with the copyright holder. This file does not set a rate, a term, or a payment schedule. Those belong in the commercial licence itself.

A commercial licence does not change the noncommercial licence for everyone else, and it does not relicense the third-party dependencies (FalkorDB, FastAPI, Next.js, and the rest). Those keep their own terms.
